RGB Vector Space

Every color is a vector from black to a point in the unit cube. Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom.

Direction = hue / which channels dominate.

Length = intensity (0 = black, 441 = white).

Components = the R, G, B edges of the dashed box.

Gray diagonal = pure luminance; distance from it = saturation.

Angle between two saved vectors = how different their hues are.
